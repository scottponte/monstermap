Using the atlas

  • Click a marker to see what the wiki knows about that place: NPCs, quests and loot. Add your own notes to any place.
  • Switch maps with the map picker at the top. Exits that lead to another mapped zone (like “To Shaded Dunes”) have a Travel button; double-clicking them works too. You arrive at the matching exit on the other side.
  • ＋ Add a map (in the map picker) adds any zone. Type its wiki page name and the app finds the map image on the wiki and loads its NPCs, quests and items. Exits that name that zone link to it automatically.
  • Right-click the map (long-press on touch) to drop a note pin right there.
  • Use the drawing tools (top-right of the map) for pins, routes, areas and circles. Click any drawing to edit its title, category, colour and notes.
  • The legend icons (banks, inns, tradeskills, notable NPCs, mob levels, respawn points) are live markers. Click one to rename it, change its type or level, add notes, or remove it.
  • ＋ Icon opens every icon. Click one, then click the map to place it. You can add your own there from a picture or an emoji; they are saved with your notes and included in Export.
  • Edit places lets you drag place markers and map icons if one is in the wrong spot.
  • Press / to search. Every place and note has a shareable link (the address bar updates as you click).
  • In notes, [[Page Name]] links to the wiki.
  • Everything is saved in this browser. Use Export to back up or share, and Import to load an export.
  • Set Leads to map on a pin to make your own link between maps.
  • Sync wiki re-downloads the current map's wiki page so new NPCs and quests show up.
